How to play

Press the start cell S and draw to the goal G. Cross only open passages. Use arrow keys as an alternative.

Drag backward to erase part of your path. Release and press the endpoint to resume. After submitting, press any cell in your route to edit its prefix, or S to start over.

Every completed route counts as an attempt. Find a perfect route, finish after a submission, or play until the 60-second deadline. Shorter routes always beat faster, longer routes.
